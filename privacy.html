Privacy Policy

OFW Custody Calendar, a Chrome extension. Last updated November 10, 2026.

In short

  • The extension has no server. Everything it reads and stores stays on your computer, in your browser.
  • It talks to one website only: OurFamilyWizard (ofw.ourfamilywizard.com), and it only reads from it. It never changes anything in your OFW account.
  • It never sees, asks for or stores your password. It uses the sign-in you already have in your browser.
  • The OFW sign-in token is kept in memory only and disappears when you close Chrome.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no remote code, and your data is never sold or shared.
  • It does not read your Google Calendar events. It only looks at the layout of the page so it can draw colored backgrounds.

What this extension is

OFW Custody Calendar shades the background of Google Calendar to show which parent has custody at each time, using the custody schedule from your OurFamilyWizard (OFW) account. It is an independent, unofficial tool. It is not made, endorsed or supported by OurFamilyWizard, LLC or by Google. "OurFamilyWizard," "OFW" and "Google Calendar" are used only to say what the extension works with.

What the extension reads and stores

All of the following is stored by Chrome on your device, in the extension's own storage. Nothing in the list is sent to the developer or to any third party.

The extension never uses Chrome's storage.sync, so none of this is copied to your Google account or other devices by the extension.

What the extension sends, and where

  • Only to OFW. The extension's only network destination is https://ofw.ourfamilywizard.com.
  • Read-only. It sends plain "read" (GET) requests to get your profile information, your calendar and a fresh sign-in token. An allow-list in the extension's code blocks any other kind of request and any other website.
  • Your own session. The requests use the OFW sign-in your browser already holds, and they ask only for your own account's information.
  • Nothing to the developer. There is no developer server, no analytics, no crash reporting and no telemetry.

Google Calendar

The extension runs a small script on calendar.google.com so it can color the page. That script:

  • reads the layout of the page (dates, hour labels, the time zone Calendar shows, and where things sit on screen), never the text or details of your events;
  • receives from the extension only what it needs to draw: the displayed parents' names and colors and the custody times for the dates on screen. It never receives your OFW token or the saved schedule as a whole;
  • makes no network requests of its own and sends nothing anywhere.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • Storage: to keep the schedule, your settings and the in-memory token described above.
  • Alarms: to run the sync on a timer. A background extension can be stopped by Chrome at any time, so a timer is needed.
  • Access to ofw.ourfamilywizard.com: to read your schedule and to pick up your sign-in token.
  • A script on calendar.google.com: to draw the colors. Chrome will warn that the extension can "read and change data" on that site because of this. It does not fetch anything from Google.

It does not ask for access to your tabs, browsing history, cookies or any other website.

Diagnostics (only if you choose)

The popup has a "Copy diagnostics" button. It puts a plain-text report on your clipboard, only when you click it, so you can paste it into a support message if you want to. The report holds counts, settings, states and the extension's own error messages. It deliberately leaves out your token, all names, user ids, colors and handoff times. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

Your account and OFW's terms

The extension uses the sign-in you already have and reads only your own account's information. You remain responsible for your OFW account and for following OFW's own Terms & Conditions and privacy policy, which apply to your use of OFW. OFW can change how its service works at any time, which may stop this extension from working. The token is a form of authentication, so treat it like a password: this extension keeps it in memory only and never shares it.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • The sign-in token is gone when you close Chrome.
  • The saved schedule is replaced at every sync and covers only the date range you choose.
  • To delete everything the extension has stored, uninstall it. Chrome removes the extension's storage when it is removed.

Limited use of data

The information the extension reads is used only to provide its single purpose: shading Google Calendar by custody schedule and showing its status. It is not sold, not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to judge creditworthiness or for lending. No person at any company reads your data, because it never leaves your device.

Children

The extension is not directed to children and does not collect information from children. Children and other non-parents in your OFW family are not needed to show custody, so the extension replaces their names with a placeholder such as "[redacted child]" as soon as it reads your family list. Their names are not saved, shown or sent anywhere. The family list OFW sends to your browser is held in memory only while a sync runs, and is not kept.

Security

The extension's saved data can be read only by the extension's own pages, not by the scripts it runs on OFW or Google Calendar, and Chrome's own protections apply. Your data is not encrypted by the extension itself beyond what your browser and operating system provide, so anyone who can use your signed-in computer account may be able to see what the extension has saved. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed before the new version is released. If a change affects what is collected or sent, the extension's store listing will say so.
